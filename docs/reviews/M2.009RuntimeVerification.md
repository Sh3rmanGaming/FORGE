## Notification Verification Scope

The passing component and integration harnesses verify:

- the frozen public creation, read, dismissal, and primitive query facade;
- deterministic validation and operation-result precedence;
- detached definitions, records, queries, events, and persistence data;
- transient, session, and savegame lifetimes;
- identifier consumption by every successful creation and non-consumption by
  failed creation;
- sequence continuity across transient gaps and hard restart;
- idempotent read and dismissal behavior;
- bounded retention, dismissed-first then read reclamation, and `STATE_ERROR`
  when safe reclamation is impossible;
- absence of silent unread-undismissed eviction;
- malformed restoration repair and complete duplicate identifier or
  `createdOrder` conflict-group rejection;
- deterministic repair independent of Lua iteration order;
- preservation of unrelated `forge.os` state and compatibility with older
  state lacking notification fields;
- repair-failure atomicity and listener-failure isolation;
- shutdown, restart, two-cycle savegame restoration, and the complete retained
  M2.003–M2.008 regression suite.
