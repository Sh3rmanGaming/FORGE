The bridge is local-process only; `player.local` persistence remains
savegame-global; stable multiplayer identity, multiplayer Host ownership,
notification authority, and dedicated-server Host/UI behaviour remain
unverified and non-frozen. No network façade transport or remote authority was
introduced.
