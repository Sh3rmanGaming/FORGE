# FORGE Project Vision

## What is FORGE?

FORGE (Farming Operations & Regional Growth Engine) is a gameplay framework for Farming Simulator 25.

Rather than adding disconnected gameplay systems, FORGE aims to create a believable professional life inside Farming Simulator.

Players begin with almost nothing and gradually build a career, qualifications, businesses, reputation and wealth through realistic projects.

---

# Mission Statement

Our goal is simple:

> Players should eventually forget they are playing a mod.

Every system should feel like it belongs naturally inside Farming Simulator.

If a feature feels "gamey", it should be redesigned until it feels believable.

---

# Design Philosophy

FORGE replaces imagination with simulation.

Instead of pretending to be:

- a contractor
- a landscaper
- a transport company
- an earthmoving company
- a farmer

FORGE provides gameplay systems that support those professions.

---

# The Five Pillars

## Communication

Everything happens through believable devices.

- Phone
- Tablet
- Laptop
- Office Computer

---

## Character

Players progress.

Not just financially.

Players gain:

- Experience
- Qualifications
- Reputation
- Relationships

---

## Business

Businesses become living organisations.

They own:

- Equipment
- Employees
- Cash
- Projects
- Workshops
- Facilities

---

## World

The world generates believable demand.

Projects appear because somebody needs work completed.

Not because RNG decided to create one.

---

## Time

FORGE uses Farming Simulator's existing time.

There is only one simulation.

No independent timers.

No offline progression.

---

# Core Principles

- Multiplayer First
- XML First
- Lua Second
- Server Authoritative
- Savegame Embedded
- Modular Architecture
- Campaign Driven
- Professional User Experience

---

# Long Term Goal

FORGE should become a platform that allows creators to build entirely new careers, campaigns and gameplay experiences without modifying the engine itself.

Campaign creators should primarily work with XML.

Lua should only be required for entirely new gameplay mechanics.

---

# Definition of Success

A player loads Farming Simulator.

After thirty minutes they stop thinking:

"I am using a mod."

And instead think:

"I own a contracting business."
